# SlovakGo A1 — Inventory derived from the 250-lesson baseline

Status: **source-derived working inventory**. This file indexes what the existing 250 A1 lessons actually teach or expose. It does not silently add grammar absent from the source.

## Quantitative baseline

- 250 lessons
- 25 sections × 10 lessons
- 1,500 vocabulary entries
- 1,272 unique Slovak expressions
- 187 expressions occur in more than one lesson
- 4,256 exercises

## CEFR interpretation

The inventory distinguishes **functional exposure** from **productive mastery**. A form may occur in a useful phrase without implying that the learner has mastered the full grammatical system.

## Grammar inventory

### Productive / clearly established in the baseline

- personal pronouns: `ja`, `ty`, `on`, `ona`, `my`, `vy`, with functional use of third-person forms
- verb `byť`: core present forms such as `som`, `si`, `je`, `sme`, `ste`, `sú`
- verb `mať`: high-frequency present forms in personal/family/possession contexts
- present-tense high-frequency verbs in routine contexts
- basic negation with `nie` and verbal `ne-`
- basic question formation and interrogatives such as `kto`, `čo`, `kde`, `odkiaľ`, `ako`, `koľko`, `kedy`
- singular/plural meaning in common nouns and pronouns
- basic grammatical gender recognition and simple adjective agreement
- possessives such as `môj/môj(a/o)`, `tvoj`, `váš/vaša` in controlled contexts
- basic past-tense communication in a dedicated past section
- basic future communication with `budem/budeš/...` in a dedicated future section
- basic modal constructions such as ability/necessity/wanting in everyday contexts
- basic comparative language in city/everyday description contexts

### Functional exposure; not full-system mastery

- accusative through high-frequency object phrases such as `mám knihu`
- locative/prepositional location such as `v Bratislave`, `v byte`
- genitive/dative/instrumental in useful chunks and contexts
- plural formation through encountered examples rather than a complete productive paradigm
- imperative through high-frequency classroom/service commands such as `čítajte`, `píšte`, `opakujte`, `pomôžte`
- reflexive verbs such as `učiť sa`, `objednať sa`, `stretnúť sa`
- verb conjugation families through individual high-frequency forms, not a complete family inventory

### Deferred from systematic A1 mastery

- full six-case productive paradigm and broad noun/adjective declension
- broad verb government by case
- systematic perfective/imperfective aspect contrast
- comprehensive reflexive morphology and valency
- full conjugation-class system
- complex subordinate syntax
- advanced register/stylistic grammatical choice

## Vocabulary architecture

Do not minimize repetition. The baseline contains 1,272 unique expressions across 1,500 entries. Repeated vocabulary is treated as useful when it follows:

**NEW → REVIEW → TRANSFER → MASTERY**

Future lessons should track vocabulary ownership and review purpose rather than banning recurrence.
