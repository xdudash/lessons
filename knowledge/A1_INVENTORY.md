# SlovakGo A1 — Current Learning Inventory

## Status
ACTIVE / planning guidance

This document describes the current A1 learning inventory for the rebuilt course. It is not a copy of the retired 250-lesson baseline and must not be treated as a fixed-content quota.

## Grammar and language functions
Current A1 design includes progressive work with:
- personal pronouns and basic identity;
- `byť` and `mať` in high-frequency present forms;
- affirmative clauses, basic questions and negation;
- common present-tense verbs for routines;
- basic gender, number and simple agreement;
- demonstratives and possessive forms in controlled contexts;
- functional object, location and movement phrases;
- numbers, quantity, time and dates;
- basic modal/request constructions;
- reflexive verbs as useful lexical chunks before full systematic mastery;
- later integrated exposure to past/future and practical survival communication.

Functional exposure is not automatically grammatical mastery. A learner may meet a case form or reflexive verb inside a useful phrase without having mastered the full grammatical system.

## Communicative progression
The current A1 roadmap builds toward the ability to:
1. introduce oneself and others;
2. give basic personal and contact information;
3. describe people, family and possessions;
4. describe home and locations;
5. describe daily actions and routines;
6. handle numbers, quantity, time and dates;
7. navigate a city and ask/give simple directions;
8. order food and interact in a café;
9. shop, discuss prices, clothing and services;
10. talk about study, work, responsibilities and free time;
11. ask for help and describe simple health problems;
12. combine A1 language in realistic everyday situations.

## Repetition policy
Vocabulary should follow purposeful:

**NEW → REVIEW → TRANSFER → MASTERY**

Repeated vocabulary is desirable when it supports retrieval and transfer. Repetition without a learning purpose is not desirable.
