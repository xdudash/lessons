```text

        ↓
byť: som / si / je / sme / ste / sú
        ↓
basic affirmative clauses
        ↓
questions and basic negation

```

```text

        ↓
demonstratives
        ↓
simple agreement
        ↓
```

## Dependency principles

1. Teach the required form or phrase before testing it productively.
2. A case form may appear as a useful chunk before the full case system is mastered.
3. A reflexive verb may be taught as a lexical chunk before systematic reflexive grammar.
4. Present-tense knowledge should anchor later time, routine and planning language.
5. Integrated lessons should recycle known language rather than silently introduce large amounts of new grammar.
6. Mastery requires transfer in a new communicative context, not recognition alone.

## A1 → A2 boundary
A2 should expand rather than restart:
- functional case chunks → systematic case paradigms and government;
- simple agreement → broader declension and agreement control;
- high-frequency verb forms → conjugation families and valency;
- practical past/future exposure → fuller tense/aspect system;
- reflexive chunks → productive reflexive morphology;
- simple coordination → broader syntax and subordination.

## Non-negotiable boundary
**Functional exposure is not grammatical mastery.**
