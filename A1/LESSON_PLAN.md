# SlovakGo A1 — Complete 80-Lesson Plan

## Purpose
This file is the detailed working plan for the complete A1 level. It is the content plan, not the lesson JSON itself.

## A1 target
- Target volume: **80 lessons**.
- Target language: **Slovak (`slovenčina`)**.
- Learner-facing instructions and explanations: **Ukrainian**.
- The 80-lesson target is a practical production target, not a reason to add filler.
- Lessons are numbered continuously `l01–l80`.
- The section determines its own lesson count; a production batch of three sections does not mean three lessons per section.

## Section 01 — Sounds, reading and basic word structure

### l01 — Prvý kontakt so slovenčinou
**Goal:** understand the learning environment and recognize basic Slovak sounds/words.
**Focus:** spelling principles, greeting forms, long vowels as recognition.
**Functions:** hello, goodbye, please, thank you.
**Mastery:** read and recognize a small set of high-frequency words and use basic courtesy expressions.

### l02 — Samohlásky a dĺžka
**Goal:** distinguish short and long vowels in familiar words.
**Focus:** a/á, e/é, i/í, o/ó, u/ú.
**Functions:** controlled reading and listening discrimination.
**Mastery:** reliably read and distinguish common vowel pairs.

### l03 — Mäkké spoluhlásky a diakritika
**Goal:** recognize č, š, ž, ď, ť, ň, ľ in common words.
**Focus:** Slovak diacritics and pronunciation patterns.
**Functions:** reading names and basic classroom words.
**Mastery:** decode common words containing Slovak-specific consonants.

### l04 — Ako čítať slovenské slovo
**Goal:** move from letter recognition to whole-word decoding.
**Focus:** syllables, stress awareness, familiar word shapes.
**Functions:** read short labels, names and simple expressions.
**Mastery:** pronounce short familiar Slovak words without spelling every letter separately.

### l05 — Pozdravy a zdvorilosť
**Goal:** use basic greeting and politeness formulas.
**Focus:** ahoj, dobrý deň, dovidenia, prosím, ďakujem.
**Functions:** informal/formal first contact.
**Mastery:** choose an appropriate basic greeting and closing for a simple situation.

### l06 — Rozumiem / nerozumiem
**Goal:** survive the first learning interaction.
**Focus:** rozumiem, nerozumiem, zopakujte, pomaly, prosím.
**Functions:** request repetition and clarification.
**Mastery:** handle a short classroom/service misunderstanding.

## Section 02 — Me, you, he/she: introductions and personal data

### l07 — Kto som?
**Goal:** introduce oneself with name and basic identity.
**Focus:** volám sa, som, kto, čo.
**Functions:** saying and asking names.
**Mastery:** exchange names in a two-turn dialogue.

### l08 — Ja, ty, on, ona
**Goal:** understand and use core personal pronouns.
**Focus:** ja, ty, on, ona, ono; basic subject meaning.
**Functions:** identify people.
**Mastery:** choose the correct pronoun in simple sentences.

### l09 — Zoznámime sa
**Goal:** introduce another person.
**Focus:** toto je, to je, kto je to?
**Functions:** introductions in a social setting.
**Mastery:** introduce oneself and one other person.

### l10 — O mne
**Goal:** give a compact personal profile.
**Focus:** som z, bývam v, hovorím po..., základné údaje.
**Functions:** origin, residence, language.
**Mastery:** answer a short personal-information interview.

### l11 — Predstavím sa
**Goal:** integrate first-contact language into a natural mini-dialogue.
**Focus:** name, origin, residence, language, polite closing.
**Functions:** self-introduction.
**Mastery:** deliver a short self-introduction without prompts.

## Section 03 — People, family and describing people

### l12 — Ľudia a vzťahy
**Goal:** name basic people and family members.
**Focus:** človek, ľudia, muž, žena, dieťa, rodina, mama, otec, brat, sestra.
**Functions:** identify people and family.
**Mastery:** describe who is present and name close relatives.

### l13 — Rodina
**Goal:** expand family vocabulary and simple family statements.
**Focus:** syn, dcéra, manžel, manželka, rodičia, bratranec, sesternica.
**Functions:** talk about family members.
**Mastery:** give a short family description.

### l14 — Môj / moja / moje v praxi
**Goal:** use basic possessive agreement with family and objects.
**Focus:** môj, moja, moje; gender awareness.
**Functions:** say whose person/thing it is.
**Mastery:** select and produce correct possessive forms in controlled phrases.

### l15 — Aký je? Aká je?
**Goal:** describe a person with basic adjectives.
**Focus:** veľký/malý, mladý/starý, dobrý, milý and simple agreement.
**Functions:** basic appearance/character description.
**Mastery:** give a 2–3 sentence description of a familiar person.

### l16 — Kto je kto?
**Goal:** combine identification, family and description.
**Focus:** kto, môj/moja, je, má, basic adjective+noun combinations.
**Functions:** identify people from a family/social scene.
**Mastery:** understand and describe relationships in a simple profile.

### l17 — Moja rodina
**Goal:** integrate the section in a realistic family conversation.
**Focus:** family vocabulary + possessives + description.
**Functions:** introduce family and answer follow-up questions.
**Mastery:** maintain a short conversation about family.

**Focus:** ne- with present-tense verbs; nie with nominal phrases.

### l36 — Môj deň od rána do večera

### l45 — Kam idem?
**Goal:** express simple movement and destination.
**Focus:** idem do..., idem na..., idem domov.

### l47 — Ako sa tam dostanem?

## Production rule for all 80 lessons
Every lesson must be built from this plan and then mapped into the known-working importer JSON contract. The plan defines **what** the lesson teaches; the lesson JSON defines **how** it is delivered.

No lesson may be reduced to a vocabulary list. New vocabulary must recur in explanations, examples, exercises and transfer. Repetition must have a purpose. Grammar may be introduced functionally before full-system mastery.

## Final A1 outcome
By the end of l80, the learner should be able to handle the main immediate A1 situations represented in the course: introduce self and others, give personal data, talk about family/home/routine, exchange numbers/time/date information, navigate a city, order and buy, discuss study/work/free time, report basic health problems, and manage short practical social interactions.
